FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SuiteCraft really free to start?

Yes. Every tool has a free tier with core features and no credit card required. Unlock Pro on any tool with a one-time payment only when you need pro limits or premium templates.

Do you store my files or data?

Most processing happens client-side in your browser. Files you upload for AI restoration or OCR are processed securely and are not retained after your session.

Is the Pro unlock a subscription?

No. Each tool's Pro unlock is a single one-time payment, not a recurring subscription. You pay once inside that tool and keep Pro access there for good.

Which file formats are supported?

Resumes export to PDF and DOCX, invoices export to PDF, the PDF suite handles merge, split, compress and OCR, and the image tool accepts JPG, PNG and WEBP.
